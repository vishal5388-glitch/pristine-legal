Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Applies to the Pristine app for iPhone and its Storage widget

The short version

Your photos, videos, contacts, calendar, bills and documents are read on your iPhone to find clutter, and they never leave it. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never sell your information.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show them, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interactions, but only if you allow it (see Ads in the free version). Pristine Pro shows no ads and runs no advertising software.

Who we are

Pristine is made by Vishal Verma ("we", "us"). You can reach us at svtechllpsupport@gmail.com.

What Pristine can access, and why

Pristine asks for each permission only for the feature that needs it. You can say no, and the other features still work.

PermissionWhat it is used for
Photos and videosFinding duplicate and similar photos, blurry or dark photos, photos and videos saved from chat apps or the web, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos; deleting the ones you choose; saving a smaller copy of a video; adding photos to, or moving them out of, the Private Vault.
Photos (bills)If you tap Find my bills, Pristine reads the text in your photos, on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition, to find photos of bills and invoices. It keeps only the details of the bills you choose to add (shop, product, date, amount, warranty length and the text, so you can search it).
Photos (IDs and cards)If you tap Find IDs & cards, Pristine reads the text in your photos on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in text recognition, to spot photos of IDs and bank cards so you can move them behind Face ID. The text is checked and thrown away. Pristine remembers only the kind of document a photo looks like, never a number or a name.
NotificationsOptional reminders before a warranty ends, and optional tips such as when your storage is filling up. Both are scheduled on your iPhone from information that stays on it. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn either off in Pristine's Settings or in iOS Settings.
Tracking (free version)Asked once, so Google can show ads that suit you. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not personalised, and nothing is linked across other apps and websites.
ContactsFinding contacts that share a name, phone number or email, and merging the ones you choose.
CalendarFinding duplicate events and events more than a year old, and deleting the ones you choose.
Face ID or passcodeUnlocking the Private Vault. iOS does the check. Pristine never receives your face data or your passcode.
Device storageShowing how much space is used and free, in the app and in the Storage widget.

All of this analysis happens on your iPhone. Photos are compared using tiny, anonymous fingerprints created and held in the phone's memory while a scan runs. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Settings: whether you finished the welcome screens, your choice for how strictly to match similar photos, and a running total of space freed and items cleaned.
  • Bills & Warranties: the details of the bills you add, stored in Pristine's private storage with iOS file protection. The bill photos themselves stay in your Photos library unless you choose to keep a protected copy inside Pristine. Removing a bill from the list does not delete the photo. Deleting the app deletes this list and any protected copies.
  • Private Vault: photos you add are kept in Pristine's private storage with iOS file protection. They are deleted if you delete the app. Move them back to Photos first if you want to keep them. If you back up your iPhone, iOS may include app data in that backup, under your own Apple settings. Pristine does not read or manage your backup.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, upload or share your photos, videos, contacts, calendar, bills, documents or any other content from your iPhone. The only data collected is by Google for ads in the free version, as described below.
  • We never use your photos, videos, contacts, calendar, bills or documents for advertising, and we do not share them with anyone.
  • We do not create accounts, so there is no account to sign in to or delete.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we hold none ourselves.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows banner ads, short video ads you can choose to watch for extra free cleans, and an occasional full-screen ad after you finish cleaning. They are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google. Pristine Pro shows no ads and does not start any advertising software.

  • What Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (worked out from your IP address), and how you interact with ads. Google uses it to choose and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. It is collected by Google, not by us, and we do not receive it.
  • Your photos and documents are never part of it. We do not give Google any content from your photos, videos, contacts, calendar, bills or documents.
  • Your permission first: before any ad is requested, iOS shows its tracking prompt and, in the EU, the UK and similar regions, Google's consent form. You can say no. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and (where it applies) in Pristine's Settings.
  • More from Google: see policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Subscriptions

Pristine Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and your billing details; we never see your card or payment information. The app learns only, through Apple's StoreKit on your device, whether you have an active subscription. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that information. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Deleting things

  • Photos and videos you delete go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos for 30 days, as with any iPhone app. iOS asks you to confirm first.
  • Calendar events deleted with Pristine are removed from your calendar and from any device that syncs it, and cannot be restored.
  • Contacts you merge are combined into one card and the duplicates are removed.

Your choices

You can turn any permission on or off at any time in Settings > Pristine. Deleting the app removes its settings and the Private Vault from your iPhone. One small number stays in your iPhone's Keychain after you delete the app: how many items you have cleaned, which is used to apply the free allowance. It never leaves your iPhone, is not synced to iCloud, and is not linked to you. Because we hold none of your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region, you have rights over personal data that Google holds for ads (access, deletion, objection); you can use them through your Google account or by writing to us, and we will pass your request on. If you have any question about your privacy, write to us.

Children

Pristine is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Ads in the free version are not shown as personalised if iOS reports that tracking is not allowed.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app first.
