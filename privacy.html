Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Applies to the Pristine app for iPhone and its Storage widget

The short version

Pristine does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. Your photos, videos, contacts and calendar are read on your iPhone to find clutter, and they never leave it.

Who we are

Pristine is made by Vishal Verma ("we", "us"). You can reach us at svtechllpsupport@gmail.com.

What Pristine can access, and why

Pristine asks for each permission only for the feature that needs it. You can say no, and the other features still work.

PermissionWhat it is used for
Photos and videosFinding duplicate and similar photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos; deleting the ones you choose; saving a smaller copy of a video; adding photos to, or moving them out of, the Private Vault.
ContactsFinding contacts that share a name, phone number or email, and merging the ones you choose.
CalendarFinding duplicate events and events more than a year old, and deleting the ones you choose.
Face ID or passcodeUnlocking the Private Vault. iOS does the check. Pristine never receives your face data or your passcode.
Device storageShowing how much space is used and free, in the app and in the Storage widget.

All of this analysis happens on your iPhone. Photos are compared using tiny, anonymous fingerprints created and held in the phone's memory while a scan runs. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Settings: whether you finished the welcome screens, your choice for how strictly to match similar photos, and a running total of space freed and items cleaned.
  • Private Vault: photos you add are kept in Pristine's private storage with iOS file protection. They are deleted if you delete the app. Move them back to Photos first if you want to keep them. If you back up your iPhone, iOS may include app data in that backup, under your own Apple settings. Pristine does not read or manage your backup.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, upload or share your photos, videos, contacts, calendar or any other personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not include third-party SDKs.
  • We do not create accounts, so there is no account to sign in to or delete.
  • We do not sell personal information, because we do not have any.

Subscriptions

Pristine Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and your billing details; we never see your card or payment information. The app learns only, through Apple's StoreKit on your device, whether you have an active subscription. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that information. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Deleting things

  • Photos and videos you delete go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos for 30 days, as with any iPhone app. iOS asks you to confirm first.
  • Calendar events deleted with Pristine are removed from your calendar and from any device that syncs it, and cannot be restored.
  • Contacts you merge are combined into one card and the duplicates are removed.

Your choices

You can turn any permission on or off at any time in Settings > Pristine. Deleting the app removes its settings and the Private Vault from your iPhone. Because we hold none of your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side, but if you have a question about your privacy, write to us.

Children

Pristine is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app first.
