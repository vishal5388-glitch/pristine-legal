Common questions

I deleted photos, but my storage did not go down.

iPhone keeps deleted photos in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted, tap Select, then Delete All to free the space right away.

Pristine says "Photo access is off" or "Limited access".

Go to Settings > Pristine > Photos and choose Full Access. With Limited Access, Pristine can only scan the photos you picked, so it will miss most duplicates.

How does Pristine decide which photo to keep?

In each set it marks the best one: a favourite first, then the highest resolution, then the largest file. The rest are pre-selected, and you can change the selection before deleting anything.

Will compressing a video lower its quality?

Pristine saves a smaller copy in a compact format, up to 1080p. It asks before deleting the original, so you can compare first. Videos that would shrink by less than 10% are skipped. Videos stored only in iCloud must be downloaded in Photos before they can be compressed.

I lost the photos in my Private Vault.

Vault photos live inside Pristine, so they are removed if the app is deleted. They are not uploaded anywhere. Before deleting the app or changing phones, open a photo in the Vault and tap Move to Photos. If your iPhone backup includes app data, restoring that backup on the same Apple account can bring the Vault back.

The Vault will not unlock.

The Vault uses Face ID or your passcode. Make sure a passcode is set in Settings > Face ID & Passcode, and that Face ID is allowed for Pristine.

Can I undo deleting calendar events?

No. Calendar deletions are permanent and sync to your other devices. Pristine only offers single events in calendars you can edit, never repeating events, and it asks you to confirm.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel my subscription?

To restore, open Pristine > Settings > Restore Purchases. To cancel, open iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Pristine Pro. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Does Pristine collect my data?

Pristine itself does not. Your photos, videos, contacts, calendar and documents are read on your iPhone and never leave it, and there are no accounts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect an advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interactions, but only if you allow it in the tracking prompt. Pristine Pro shows no ads. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I stop personalised ads or remove the ads?

To limit personalisation, open iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn Pristine off. To remove ads completely, upgrade to Pristine Pro in Pristine > Settings.
